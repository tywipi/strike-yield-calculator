04 / The Math

No black box. Here's the formula.

Every number on this page comes from this calculation. Challenge any step — that's the point.

Annual yield = Daily Volume × 365 × Net Fee Rate × ( Your STRIKE ÷ Total Staked ) Net Fee Rate = Tiered taker fee − Maker rebate (compresses from 0.035% → 0.024% as volume scales)
  • V2 data only. V1 revenue exists and pays stakers too, but won't scale like V2 — excluding it keeps the model conservative. Whatever V1 pays is bonus.
  • Fee rate confirmed from dashboard: $24,739 revenue ÷ $69,627,002 volume = 0.03554% blended.
  • Fee compression applied. As volume grows, traders qualify for lower fee tiers (Tier 0: 0.080% → Tier 6: 0.028%). The blended rate falls accordingly.
  • Maker rebates subtracted. Pro market makers earn rebates (up to −0.010%), reducing net revenue to stakers at scale.
  • Staking participation: 84.5% of circulating supply (≈19.93M of 23.59M STRIKE). Non-custodial — tokens never leave your wallet.
  • Known unknown: staked-STRIKE fee discounts may further compress staker revenue. Not yet confirmed — treat these numbers as the pre-discount baseline.
⚠ Not financial advice. This is a community-built model anchored to publicly visible Strike Finance V2 dashboard data. Yield fluctuates with volume, staking participation, and fee structure changes.
